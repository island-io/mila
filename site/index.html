Release notes and earlier versions

Everything happens on your Mac

No account, no subscription, no upload. Mila runs Whisper models locally on the Mac's GPU.

  • On-device transcription

    Whisper runs through whisper.cpp on Apple's Metal GPU: OpenAI's large-v3-turbo for English and other languages, and the ivrit.ai large-v3 model for Hebrew.

  • Record any meeting

    Capture Zoom, Google Meet in Chrome, Slack or another app together with your microphone. No virtual audio device to install.

  • Know who said what

    Speaker diarization labels each voice. Name a speaker once and Mila keeps the name for later recordings. Needs an Apple Silicon Mac.

  • Live transcript

    Watch the transcript appear while the meeting is still going, on Macs fast enough to keep up in real time.

  • Dictate anywhere

    Press ⌘2 for English or ⌘3 for Hebrew, speak, and the text is pasted where your cursor is. Both shortcuts can be changed.

  • Works with your AI tools

    Ask Claude about your meetings through Mila's built-in MCP server, or get titles and summaries from the AI tool you already use. Both stay off until you turn them on.

  • Share with a colleague

    Send one .milashare file with the audio, transcript, speaker names and summary. It opens straight into their Mila.

  • iPhone voice memos

    Voice Memos synced from your iPhone are transcribed automatically. Export subtitles as SRT, or copy the text anywhere.

For teams and IT

Transcription for your whole company, for free

Mila is open source under the Apache 2.0 license. There are no seats, no per-minute fees and no vendor holding your recordings. Your only cost is the hardware you choose to run it on.

  • Nothing to run at all. On Apple Silicon Macs every recording is transcribed locally, so there is no server to buy or maintain.
  • Or one shared GPU server. Point everyone's Mila at a transcription server you host. Any OpenAI-compatible /v1/audio/transcriptions endpoint works, and audio stays on infrastructure you control.
  • Ready-made deployment. The repository ships Kubernetes manifests for a speaches server on a single GPU node, a Docker image with the ivrit.ai Hebrew model built in, and a single-machine quickstart.
  • One-click rollout. Hand teammates a .milaconfig file. Double-clicking it sets the server, models and language in one step.

System requirements

macOS
macOS 14 Sonoma or later.
Mac
Apple Silicon (M-series) strongly recommended. Intel Macs can transcribe, on the CPU and much more slowly, but speaker labels and voice recognition need Apple Silicon.
Memory
16 GB recommended. 8 GB is workable for plain transcription.
Disk
About 4.6 GB for the English and Hebrew models, downloaded on first launch. About 1 GB more for speaker labels.
