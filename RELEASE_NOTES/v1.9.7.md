# Mila 1.9.7

**New**

- **Share a recording with another Mila user.** Right-click a recording and choose "Share Recording…" to export it as a single `.milashare` file carrying the audio, transcript, speaker names, summary, action items and, if you choose to include them, the speakers' voice profiles. The recipient double-clicks or drops the file onto Mila and confirms what gets imported. Nothing has to be re-transcribed or re-named. Contributed by Daniel Solomon (@DanielSolomon).
- **Record now, transcribe after the call.** A new "Transcribe while recording" switch in Settings → Audio (on by default) lets you turn off live transcription, live speaker labels and Live AI for the duration of a call so Mila stays off the CPU; the recording is transcribed in the background once you stop. You can also stop a running transcription from the queue and run it later without losing the recording. Contributed by Ori Seri (@oriser).
- **Trim a transcript.** Click a line to select it (Shift-click for a range, Command-click to add or remove one), then right-click to delete the selected lines, or choose "End Conversation Here" to drop every line after it and cut the audio there too, which helps when Mila kept recording after the meeting ended. Both ask first, since neither can be undone, and the summary is regenerated afterwards when summaries are on.
- **Mila suggests speaker names.** Set "Your name" in Settings → Speakers and Mila offers it for the single voice in a recording from your microphone or a Voice Memo. With the new "Speaker names" option in Settings → AI Features, Mila also asks your AI provider which unnamed speakers say their name ("Hi, I'm Dana") or are addressed by name. Suggestions appear above the transcript with Accept and Dismiss, and nothing is renamed until you accept one.
- **Start the next recording straight away.** After you press Stop, the Record button no longer sits on "Finalizing…" while Mila finishes the previous recording's live transcript. A new recording starts capturing immediately, and the previous one finishes in the background.
- **Choose the summary language per recording.** The AI summary and action items can now be regenerated in English or Hebrew for a single recording from its context menu, the detail view or the rename sheet, overriding the global Output language in Settings → AI Features. An English summary of a Hebrew recording now renders left-to-right. Contributed by Daniel Solomon (@DanielSolomon).
- **The Record button shows "Starting…" while the microphone comes up.** With AirPods on macOS 27 the microphone can take several seconds to start; the button now shows that it is working instead of looking idle, and a second click no longer starts a second capture. Cancelling a start, or the Mac sleeping or locking during one, no longer opens the microphone or system audio.
- **Organizations can manage Mila's settings.** An IT administrator can now use a configuration profile to set the same options a `.milaconfig` file carries: the transcription server, recording language, Live AI, speaker labels and meeting detection. Those settings show "Managed by your organization" in Settings and can't be changed there.
- **Claude can get timestamped transcripts and save them to files.** The `mila-mcp` helper's `get_transcript` tool accepts `format: "srt"`, so a Claude session can produce subtitles or answer "when was that said". A new `save_transcript` tool writes a transcript straight to a file instead of Claude retyping it, which takes seconds instead of minutes for a long Hebrew meeting. Saving files is a separate permission, off until you turn on "Also allow saving transcripts to files" in Settings → Storage. Contributed by Daniel Solomon (@DanielSolomon).

**Fixes**

- **A network drop no longer interrupts a remote transcription.** When the transcription server can't be reached during a recording, Mila keeps recording and shows a banner instead of an error alert. The live transcript catches up once the connection is back, and anything still missing when you stop is transcribed from the recorded audio, so nothing said during the outage is lost.
- **Fixed a crash while recording a meeting.** Mila now checks the call audio it receives from macOS before reading it, and skips a malformed packet instead of crashing. Contributed by @noamleibovitch.
- **No more crash when quitting during a remote transcription.** Quitting while a recording was uploading to a remote transcription server could abort Mila. Uploads are now cancelled cleanly on quit and the unfinished recording is picked up again on the next launch. Contributed by @noamleibovitch.
- **Removing a known speaker forgets their voice.** The ✕ next to a name in Settings → Speakers → Known speakers used to remove only the name, so Mila kept recognising that voice and labelling it with the removed name in new recordings. It now deletes the voice profile too, after asking. Contributed by Daniel Solomon (@DanielSolomon).
- **A failed or empty re-transcription keeps your transcript.** If re-transcribing a recording fails, returns no text, or its audio is rejected as too short or too quiet, Mila now keeps the earlier transcript with its original language and model, and tells you why. Stopping a re-transcription restores it the same way, even after a relaunch.
- **Deleting a recording stops its AI work.** Permanently deleting a recording or emptying Recently Deleted now stops its summary and any follow-up AI calls, ends the Claude CLI and anything it started, and no longer shows a "summary finished" notice for a recording that is gone.
- **A summary requested while one is running is no longer dropped.** Mila runs it again as soon as the first finishes.
- **Mila tells you if your voice profiles could not be read.** A damaged voice-profiles file is now kept as a backup instead of being overwritten. Mila tells you once in the main window and in Settings → Speakers, with a "Show in Finder" button so you can recover it.
- **Claude integration fixes.** The `mila-mcp` helper no longer crashes on recordings with unusual durations, and SRT transcripts keep one cue per segment.
