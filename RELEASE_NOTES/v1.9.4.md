# Mila 1.9.4

**Fixes**

- **Recording no longer keeps Mila busy at 60–75% CPU.** Every recording — including ones transcribed by a remote server, with nothing to compute locally — pinned Mila's main thread for its whole duration. The level meters and the elapsed clock were re-rendering the entire app, sidebar and menu bar on every audio buffer. They now update only the small views that display them; a recording on the remote backend runs at a few percent, and the Settings → Audio level meter no longer does the same while it is open.
