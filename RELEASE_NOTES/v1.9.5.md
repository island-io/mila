# Mila 1.9.5

**New**

- **Mila has a new app icon.** The Island mark with a microphone badge, redrawn as vector artwork so it stays sharp everywhere the icon appears — the Dock, Finder, Spotlight and the Get Info panel — and sized to Apple's icon grid so it sits at the same visual weight as every other app in your Dock.
- **Playback speed on the recording screen.** A new speed control at the right end of the playback bar plays a recording back anywhere from 0.5× to 2×, in quarter steps — so an hour-long meeting takes half an hour to review, and a passage that's hard to make out can be slowed down. Your choice is remembered across recordings and across launches, and voices keep their natural pitch at every speed. Picking a speed while paused just sets it; it doesn't start playing.
- **The transcript now follows playback.** The highlighted line used to scroll out of view within a few seconds, leaving you dragging the transcript to keep up with the audio — on a long recording the highlight was usually somewhere off screen. It now keeps the current line in view as the recording plays. Scrolling yourself takes over, and a **Follow playback** button appears to hand control back; tapping any line does the same. Resizing the window no longer counts as scrolling away.

**Fixes**

- **Recording works again with AirPods connected on macOS 27.** Pressing Record while AirPods (or another Bluetooth headset) were the Mac's audio output failed every time with *"The operation couldn't be completed (Mila.MicrophoneError error 1)"*. macOS 27 takes about six seconds to negotiate the headset's audio format, and Mila gave up after five — one second before the microphone was ready. Mila now waits up to 12 seconds, so the recording simply starts once the mic is up. Switching Mila's input to the built-in microphone did not help, because the delay comes from the headset being the *output*; the same change covers that case. On an affected Mac the Record button can take a few seconds to respond while macOS talks to the headset — that's expected.
- **A clearer message when a microphone really won't start.** Instead of an error code, the banner now names the microphone, says how long Mila waited, and points you to Settings → Audio to pick a different input.

The playback speed control and transcript auto-scroll come from a contribution by [@DanielSolomon](https://github.com/DanielSolomon) — thank you.
