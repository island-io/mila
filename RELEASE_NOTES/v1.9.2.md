# Mila 1.9.2

The stable release of everything in the 1.9.2 betas, plus the fixes that came out of testing them.

**New**

- **Pause and resume a recording.** Long meetings have stretches you don't want captured at all — a side conversation, a break, someone reading a password out loud. Pause leaves that span genuinely absent from the audio rather than recorded as silence, and the timer stops while you're paused.
- **Microsoft Teams meetings are detected**, alongside Zoom. Mila offers to start recording when a Teams call begins and offers to stop when it ends, using the same reliable audio signal it uses for Zoom rather than guessing from window titles.
- **Send your transcripts to Obsidian.** Point Mila at a vault in Settings → Storage and every finished recording is written there as a Markdown note — title, date, duration, summary, action items, transcript. Off by default; nothing happens until you choose a vault.
- **Bring your own AI provider — no local CLI required.** Summaries, action items and recording names can now talk to any OpenAI-compatible endpoint: OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, DeepSeek, Ollama Cloud, a local Ollama, or your own server. The existing Claude and Cursor CLI options are untouched, and **Gemini CLI** joins them.
- **Empty Trash.** Clear everything in Recently Deleted in one action instead of deleting rows one at a time. It tells you how many recordings it will delete and asks first.
- **AI settings are reorganised into two tabs.** *AI Provider* is where you configure your AI once; *AI Features* is where you turn each feature on and edit its prompt, one expandable section per feature. Your existing provider, API key and prompts carry over untouched.

**Transcription**

- **English recordings on a Hebrew server no longer come back with Hebrew words in them.** If your transcription server runs a Hebrew model like ivrit.ai, Settings → Models now has an **English model** field so English recordings go to a multilingual model while Hebrew keeps using the accurate Hebrew one. Leave it blank if your server already handles every language.
- **The recording language is Hebrew or English — Auto-detect is gone.** Mila picks which speech model to use *from* that choice, so "let it detect" meant guessing which specialist to ask before knowing what language it needed to handle. Picking the language in the toolbar is one click and gives a noticeably better transcript.
- **Transcription progress no longer sticks or jumps backwards.** The progress bar now only ever moves forward, and the app stays responsive during long transcriptions.

**Recording reliability**

- **Unplugging your headphones mid-meeting no longer silently stops the recording.** If the microphone Mila was recording from went away — a wired headset unplugged, a Bluetooth headset switching profile, another app taking the device — capture used to die on the spot while the timer kept counting: one user's 26-minute call was saved as 24 seconds of audio. Mila now notices within seconds and picks the recording back up on whichever microphone is available.
- **Dictation and recording no longer capture silence on some Macs.** A repair meant to recover from that device change could retrigger itself on certain audio setups, restarting capture several times a second so nothing was ever recorded — and the failure surfaced as a confusing "failed to decode" error from the transcription server. Fixed, with the microphone indicator no longer flickering.
- **Recordings recovered after a crash now transcribe.** A crash or force-quit left the audio file's header unfinished, so every reader — including Mila's own transcription — saw an empty file even though the audio was all there on disk. Those recordings are repaired on the next launch.
- **Renaming, filing or deleting a recording while it transcribes now sticks.** Those edits used to be silently reverted when the transcription finished.
- **App audio comes back on its own** if macOS drops the other side of a meeting part way through, and a recording that captured far less audio than the time you spent recording now says so plainly instead of leaving you to discover it in the transcript.
- **A recording's length comes from the audio itself**, so a capture that ended early no longer claims the full duration.

**Fixes**

- **The live transcript stays with the newest words** instead of drifting out of view and needing to be scrolled by hand.
- **Stopping a recording really stops the AI.** The Live AI session could fire one more request after Stop and leave a temporary folder behind.
- **All Settings tabs are reachable.** With ten tabs the window was too narrow, so the last ones collapsed into a `>>` menu and looked disabled. The Updates tab has also moved into **General** — its automatic-update and beta options are there now.
- **Beta builds are no longer offered to people who didn't ask for them.** A pre-release published without its channel marker was offered to everyone; Mila now refuses pre-release updates unless you've enabled beta versions in Settings → General.
