# Mila 1.9.8

**Fixes**

- **Mila no longer quits when you stop a recording.** In 1.9.7, pressing Stop with speaker labels on could close Mila without warning. Your recording was kept and finished transcribing on the next launch, but the app should never have closed. It now stops normally, even when the speaker-detection helper has failed in the background.
- **Speaker detection works again.** 1.9.7 shipped with a mismatched Python component, so no speakers were detected: there were no live speaker labels during a recording and no speaker separation after it. Speaker labels, known-speaker recognition and speaker name suggestions all work again. Nothing to reinstall: update Mila, and the next recording is diarized as before.
