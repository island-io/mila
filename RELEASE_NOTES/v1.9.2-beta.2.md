# Mila 1.9.2-beta.2

- **Unplugging your headphones mid-meeting no longer silently stops the recording.** If the microphone Mila was recording from went away — USB EarPods or a wired headset unplugged, a Bluetooth headset switching profile, another app taking the device — macOS quietly swapped in a different mic and Mila's capture died on the spot. The timer kept counting and the recording looked normal in the list, but nothing after that moment was actually recorded: one user's 26-minute Zoom call was saved as 24 seconds of audio. Mila now notices within a few seconds and picks the recording back up on whichever microphone is available, so you lose a moment instead of the meeting. The same watchdog also covers a microphone that stops delivering audio for any other reason.
- **App audio (the other side of a meeting) comes back on its own if macOS drops it.** When the system audio capture died part way through a recording, Mila silently continued with just your own microphone. It now restarts that capture and writes down what happened, so a half-captured meeting is something you can see rather than something you discover in the transcript.
- **Recordings show how long they actually are.** A recording's length came from the timer that ran while you recorded, so a capture that ended early still claimed the full length — a 24-second file listed as 26:24. The length now comes from the audio itself.
- **Capture that ends early tells you so.** If a recording captures far less audio than the time you spent recording, Mila now says that plainly — and suggests pinning a specific input in Settings → Audio Input — instead of leaving you to guess why the transcript stops in the middle.
